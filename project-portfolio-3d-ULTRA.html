📋 Project Description

This is more than a portfolio, it's a piece of my life converted into code. An immersive experience that combines technology and creativity to showcase my projects in a unique and interactive way.

Developed from scratch with Three.js, it includes an innovative navigation system that allows exploring projects and skills in a completely immersive way with optimized models, dynamic lighting, and custom shaders.

🚀 Main Features

  • Immersive 3D Navigation: Freely navigate the 3D model with intuitive controls
  • Interactive Portfolio: Explore projects with smooth transitions and animations
  • Personal Information: Access my story, experience, and professional journey
  • Professional Services: Discover the services I offer with specific details
  • Contact System: Contact me directly from the integrated platform
  • Performance Optimization: Progressive asset loading and mobile optimization
  • Responsive Experience: Perfect adaptation to all devices
  • Credits and References: Complete access to project documentation and references
