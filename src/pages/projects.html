Interactive 3D Portfolio
This is more than a portfolio, it's a piece of my life converted into code. Every line of Three.js reflects my passion for creating experiences that connect with people. I developed this 3D world because I believe technology should be human, emotional, and memorable. Here I don't just show my technical skills, but also my way of seeing development: as an art that combines creativity, innovation, and a personal touch that makes a difference.
Three.js Blender WebGL GSAP JavaScript GLSL
Complete Description: This portfolio represents the evolution from traditional presentation to an immersive 3D experience. Developed from scratch with Three.js, it includes an innovative navigation system that allows users to explore my projects and skills interactively. The implementation includes custom shaders, dynamic lighting, and mobile device optimizations.
Technologies: Three.js, WebGL, CSS3DRenderer, JavaScript ES6, GLSL Shaders, Webpack
  • Real-time 3D rendering with performance optimization
  • Intuitive navigation system with smooth transitions
  • Interactive 3D models with basic physics
  • Fluid animations with CSS3D and GSAP
  • Responsive design adapted for mobile and tablets
  • Progressive asset loading system
  • Optimized cross-browser compatibility
